From questions to evidence.

The module emphasizes sound public health reasoning: define the question, choose the right summary or method, quantify uncertainty, and communicate what the result does—and does not—show.

Simulated by design.
专为教学而模拟。

Every record, figure, and result in this learning module is generated from a fixed random seed for education and demonstration. The site contains no real patient records, personal health information, or operational data.

本学习模块中的所有记录、图表和结果均由固定随机种子生成，仅用于教学与演示。网站不包含真实患者记录、个人健康信息或运营数据。
