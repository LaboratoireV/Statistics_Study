Learn the reasoning—not just the command.

These modules are designed to make statistical choices inspectable. They connect the research question, target quantity, assumptions, code, diagnostics, uncertainty, and final interpretation. 课程强调可检查的统计推理：把研究问题、目标量、假设、代码、诊断、不确定性与最终解释连成一体。

Reproducible by defaultVisible R code, fixed seeds, and complete worked examples.
Assumptions made explicitDiagnostics and limitations accompany every major method.
Safe teaching dataAll records and findings are simulated; no patient information is used.
